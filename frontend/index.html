About Channapatna Toys

🪵 Gombegala Ooru — The Toy City of Karnataka

Known as Gombegala Ooru (Toy City) of Karnataka, Channapatna has a 200-year-old heritage of wooden toy making. Awarded Geographical Indication (GI Tag No. 18), these toys are crafted exclusively from sustainably harvested Hale Mara (Wrightia tinctoria) and colored using 100% natural vegetable lacquers (turmeric, kumkum, indigo).

This AI-Powered Platform was created to bridge traditional artisan craft economics with modern marketplace intelligence, preventing intermediary wage depreciation and protecting 3,000+ craft families across Karnataka.
